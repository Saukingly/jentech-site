What we collect

We collect information you provide directly to us:

  • Account information — name, email address, phone number, and company name, when you create a client portal account or submit a contact/request form.
  • Project information — details you share about a project, service request, or inquiry.
  • Documents — reports, test results, and other files exchanged through the client portal in connection with work we do for you.

We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18, and our services are directed at businesses and professionals, not the general public.

How we use it

  • To provide the engineering, geotechnical, and materials-testing services you request
  • To communicate with you about your project, account, or inquiry
  • To maintain your client portal account and the documents shared through it
  • To meet our own legal, accounting, and record-keeping obligations

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising or share it with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

Your rights

Under Jamaica's Data Protection Act, you have the right to:

  • Ask us what personal information we hold about you
  • Ask us to correct inaccurate information
  • Ask us to delete your account and associated personal information, subject to our legal and record-keeping obligations
  • Withdraw consent for us to process your information going forward
  • Object to how your information is being used

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details below.

Cookies

Our client portal and admin systems use a session cookie to keep you logged in. This is functionally necessary — without it, the site can't tell you're logged in between page loads. We don't use tracking or advertising cookies.
